Platform Mechanics

How SkillSwap Works

A transparent, fair, credit-based peer learning model designed for students.

Phase 1

1. Build Your Profile

List skills you're confident teaching (e.g. Python, Video Editing) and skills you're eager to learn (e.g. UI/UX Design, Public Speaking).

Phase 2

2. Intelligent Matching

Our matching algorithm searches for reciprocal pairs (where A teaches what B wants, and B teaches what A wants) or direct peer tutors.

Phase 3

3. Peer Sessions

Send a learning request, select a convenient date, and meet in our in-app room to practice practical skills together.

Phase 4

4. Credits & Progress

Completing a teaching session earns you 5 credits. Your Personal Skill Map updates automatically as your mastery increases.

Understanding the Credit-Based Exchange System

SkillSwap utilizes an internal token system called Knowledge Credits to keep skill sharing completely equitable and free of financial hurdles.

+15 Starter Credits

Every newly registered student receives 15 complimentary credits upon onboarding to book their first 3 peer sessions immediately.

+5 Credits for Teaching

Whenever you conduct a learning session and help a peer student, 5 credits are automatically credited to your balance.

-5 Credits for Learning

Accessing a peer session costs 5 credits, incentivizing students to also give back and share their own knowledge.
