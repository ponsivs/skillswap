Our Vision

Empowering Students Through Collaborative Knowledge

SkillSwap was founded on a simple truth: "Everyone has something valuable to teach. Everyone has something new to learn."

Our Mission

Make practical, hands-on skill development accessible to every student by connecting peers who possess complementary skill sets. We remove high tuition costs and replace them with peer-to-peer exchange powered by learning credits.

Our Vision

Create a global learning ecosystem where college and school students build verified portfolios, gain confidence as mentors, and track their intellectual evolution through dynamic, interactive skill maps.

Safety & Privacy Standard

Built with Student Privacy & Protection at Heart

As SkillSwap expands to college and school communities, maintaining a safe, supervised, and respectful environment is our non-negotiable priority:

  • No Public Personal Details: Student phone numbers and personal email addresses are never exposed publicly on profiles.
  • Controlled Platform Environment: All session planning, credit exchanges, and feedback remain contained within SkillSwap.
  • Community Moderation & Reporting: Any member can report suspicious behavior with one click, triggering rapid review by platform administrators.
  • Institutional Communities: Designed to support institution-verified networks and teacher-supervised groups for younger students.
Zero Data Selling

Student learning data is strictly protected and never sold to third-party ad networks.
